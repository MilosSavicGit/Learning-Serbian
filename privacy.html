Privacy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

In short. Pomalo is free and has no ads, no tracking and no selling of data. We store a parent’s or adult learner’s email address, the first names the learners choose, and their progress, only so that learning can continue on any device. You can delete a learner or your whole account yourself, at any time, inside Pomalo.

Ukratko. Pomalo je besplatan, bez reklama, bez praćenja i bez prodaje podataka. Čuvamo email roditelja ili odraslog učenika, imena koja učenici izaberu i njihov napredak, samo da bi učenje moglo da se nastavi na bilo kom uređaju. Učenika ili ceo nalog možeš sam da obrišeš u svakom trenutku, u Pomalu.

Who runs Pomalo

Pomalo is made by Miloš Savić. For any question about your data, or to ask for a copy or a correction, write to contact@bymilossavic.com.

What we store

  • Email address of the parent or adult learner, used to sign in with a one-time code.
  • Learners: the first name or nickname you type (a real surname is never needed), the course direction, Ekavian or Ijekavian, Latin or Cyrillic.
  • Progress: finished lessons, stars, points, A1 test results, and which sentences were answered wrongly, so Pomalo can practise them again.
  • Activity: dates of registration and visits, and when a lesson or test was finished.
  • Sign-in records kept by our database provider for security, such as sign-in times, IP address and browser type.

We do not collect location, photos, contacts, microphone or camera recordings, advertising IDs or analytics. Pomalo has no ads and no tracking cookies.

Stored on your device

The browser keeps a copy of the current learner’s progress and settings (in “local storage”, not cookies), so Pomalo starts quickly and works with a weak connection. Signing out or deleting the account removes it.

Children

Children’s profiles are created and managed by a parent or guardian under the parent’s own email address. Children never need an email address of their own, and we only ask for a first name or nickname. A parent can see, change or delete their children’s profiles at any time. If you believe a child has been registered without a parent’s permission, write to us and we will delete the profile.

Who can see the data

  • You: a parent sees only their own learners. Database rules prevent anyone from reading another family’s data.
  • The course administrator (Miloš Savić) can see learners’ names, progress and mistakes, to keep the course working, improve sentences that many learners find hard, and pause access in case of misuse.
  • We never sell or share the data with advertisers or anyone else.

Some of these providers may process data outside your country, including outside the EU. They do so under their data protection agreements and, where needed, the EU’s standard contractual clauses.

Why we use the data

Only to provide what you asked for: an account, saved progress on any device, practice of past mistakes, and the parent or administrator overview. For children, we rely on the parent who creates the profile.

How long we keep it

As long as the account exists. When you delete a learner or the account, the data is removed from the database straight away. Sign-in records kept by our providers for security are deleted by them on their own schedules.

How to delete your data

  • In Pomalo: sign in, and on the “Who’s learning today?” screen open Account and privacy. There you can delete one learner, or your whole account with all learners.
  • By email: write to contact@bymilossavic.com from the account’s email address, and we will delete it for you.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion, and you can object to how it is used. If you are in the EU, you can also complain to your national data protection authority.

Changes

If this page changes in an important way, we will update the date at the top and tell learners in Pomalo.
