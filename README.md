# Pomalo · learning Serbian little by little

Pomalo means "little by little". It is a playful course in Serbian and English, guided by Vrapko the sparrow, who cheers every right answer and sings when an exercise is done. It is made for children, with big buttons, pictures and short steps they can follow on their own, and it works just as well for adult beginners.

**Completely free:** no payments, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases. It runs in any web browser today, and an Android app will follow once testing is finished.

**Try it:** https://milossavicgit.github.io/Learning-Serbian/

## What's inside

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The whole app in one file: 13 lessons, Vrapko, pictures, songs, stars, mistake review |
| `content/` | Lesson sentences as JSON (Lessons 2–6 and 11–13), for the future Supabase version |
| `docs/course-plan.md` | Plan for Lessons 11–48, taken from the notebook covers |
| `tools/heic-to-jpeg.html` | Converts iPhone HEIC photos to JPEG in the browser |

## Features

- **Two directions:** English → Serbian, and Serbian → English
- **Both variants and scripts:** Ekavian or Ijekavian, Latin or Cyrillic, chosen once at registration
- **Difficulty that grows:** choose the translation → choose the missing word → type the missing word → translate the whole sentence
- **Mistake review:** every new session starts by practising earlier mistakes
- **Vrapko the sparrow:** cheers, encourages, and sings a short song after each exercise
- **Pictures:** 91 cartoon pictures of food, animals and everyday things
- **Progress map:** one dot per lesson, with 1–3 stars for each finished lesson
- **A1 test:** 15 questions from all of Level 1; 12 correct answers open Level 2 (A2)
- **Fair answer checking:** synonyms, word order, missing č/ć/š/ž/đ, Cyrillic or Latin, and names are all accepted, and a spelling slip gets a second try
- **Serbian voice:** every Serbian sentence can be heard, read by Nikola

## Run it

**On your computer:** download `index.html` and double-click it.

**Online with GitHub Pages (free):**
1. In this repository, open **Settings → Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, set **Source** to **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Choose branch **main** and folder **/ (root)**, then click **Save**.
4. After a minute or two, the app is live at `https://milossavicgit.github.io/Learning-Serbian/`.

## Accounts and progress

Learners sign in with their email address and a 6-digit code, from any device. One parent account can hold several learners, and adults sign up the same way. Progress, stars and mistakes are saved to the learner's account, so they continue where they stopped on any phone, tablet or computer.
